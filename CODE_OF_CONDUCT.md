# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in Mercury a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy, kindness, and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive, evidence-based feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- handling network evidence, vulnerability reports, and personal data responsibly;
- respecting authorization boundaries and never pressuring others to test systems they do not own or have permission to assess.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment, intimidation, threats, or stalking;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- using project spaces to coordinate unauthorized scanning, disruption, credential attacks, or other harmful activity;
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, community events, and private project communications.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this Code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through a private channel made available by the repository host. Do not use public issues for sensitive conduct reports. This repository does not currently publish a dedicated conduct email address; do not include secrets or vulnerability details in a conduct report. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and apply responses proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Anyone enforcing this Code must recuse themselves when they have a conflict.

## Enforcement guidelines

Depending on context, severity, history, and impact, maintainers may use:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation of the violation and the expected change.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited ban from interaction or participation.
4. **Permanent restriction** — permanent removal from project participation for sustained, severe, or repeated misconduct.
